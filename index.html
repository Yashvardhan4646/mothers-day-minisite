Dear Mom,

2 min read
Mother's Day, 2026

I've been thinking about how to write this for a while now. How do you put into words something that fills every quiet corner of your heart? How do you thank someone for being the reason the world feels safe?

I think about you in the strangest moments — when I smell something warm baking, when a song comes on that you used to hum, when I catch myself saying something you would say and I smile before I even realize it. You are woven into every good part of me.

You taught me how to be kind. Not by lecturing, but by the way you always made space for others — even when you were tired, even when no one noticed. You showed me that love isn't loud; it's consistent. It's showing up. It's staying.

I know I haven't always said it enough. Life moves fast, and "thank you" feels too small for what you've given me. But I want you to know: every time I have chosen patience over frustration, chosen to stay when leaving would have been easier, chosen to believe in someone — I learned that from watching you.

You are the reason I believe in warmth. In gentleness. In the power of a quiet presence. The world is better because you are in it, and I am better because you are mine.

So today, and every day after, I just want to say — thank you. Thank you for every sacrifice I didn't see. For every prayer you whispered in the dark. For every time you put me first without making me feel guilty for it.

I love you more than I will ever be able to say.

With all my heart,
Your Child
P.S. — You've always been my favorite. Don't tell the others.
